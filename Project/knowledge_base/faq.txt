Sales Assistant FAQ

Q: What information should be extracted from a website lead?
A: Extract contact information, company, job title, company size when available, industry, product interest, use case, pain points, budget signals, timeline, purchase intent, urgency, source, language, requirements and missing information.

Q: What does a high-priority lead mean?
A: A high-priority lead has several strong buying signals such as a specific requirement, clear purchase intent, suitable company profile, a defined timeline, a demo request or an indication of budget.

Q: Should the AI make the final sales decision?
A: No. The AI should assist the sales team by prioritizing leads, explaining signals and recommending next actions. A salesperson remains responsible for the final decision.

Q: What if information is missing?
A: The system should explicitly identify missing information rather than inventing values.

Q: Can the assistant process multilingual leads?
A: Yes. The assistant can detect the lead language, extract structured information and provide a response in the lead's language when supported by the configured language model.

Q: What should happen after qualification?
A: The system should present the score, priority, reasons, relevant product information, missing information and a recommended next action to the sales team.
