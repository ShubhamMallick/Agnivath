Example Pricing Information

Suitable for small organizations and basic use cases.

Professional AI Solution:
Suitable for growing organizations requiring multiple AI workflows.

Designed for organizations with large employee bases, complex requirements and enterprise deployment needs.

- Number of documents processed

- Deployment requirements
- Security requirements
