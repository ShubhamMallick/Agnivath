Sales Lead Qualification Rules

HIGH PRIORITY:
A lead should generally be considered high priority when several of the following conditions are present:
- Clear purchase intent
- Specific business requirement
- Large organization
- Budget is available or strongly implied
- Specific implementation timeline
- Demo or sales call requested
- Decision-maker or senior technology/business role

MEDIUM PRIORITY:
A lead may be medium priority when:
- There is a clear business problem
- The company appears to fit the target customer
- The customer is researching solutions
- The implementation timeline is unclear
- Budget information is unavailable

LOW PRIORITY:
A lead may be low priority when:
- The person is only browsing
- There is no clear business requirement
- The person is only requesting general information
- There is no identifiable business use case

IMPORTANT:
The qualification system should not make a final decision solely from the job title or company size. The lead's actual message, requirements, intent and timeline should be considered.
